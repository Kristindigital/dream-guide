# The DREAM Engine

Go from idea to a launched digital product in 30 days, without tech skills. The DREAM Engine is Kristin's DREAM Method as a guide that works with you inside Claude: **Define, Research, Execute, Apply, Market.** It researches what sells, writes your pages and builds your product in your Canva. You just make the decisions.

## Use it

Start a chat in your DREAM project and say **"Let's start my DREAM"** (or type `/` and pick **dream**).
Your guide asks a few simple questions, does the research and the drafting for you, and stops for your OK at the big moments. Come back any time and say **"Keep going"** to pick up exactly where you left off, or **"Where am I?"** to see your progress.

## Your business notes

Everything you decide is kept in one file, `my-dream-business.md`, so you never have to repeat yourself.
- In a Claude project, your guide keeps it up to date in the project's Context for you.
- In Cowork, it's saved in the folder you work in.

## Updates

When there's a new version, update it in **Customize › Plugins**: click **+ Add**, then **Manage marketplaces**. Click the **⋮** next to **dream-guide**, choose **Check for updates**, then **Done**. The first time you use it after, it tells you what's new. Type **/update** any time to see what's new.

## Data

Your guide uses Claude's own web search for research. Your notes stay in your own folder or your own Claude project. Nothing is sent to Kristin.
