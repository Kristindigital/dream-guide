# What's new in the DREAM Engine

Newest version first. Written for students: plain words, 2 to 4 lines per version.
This is the only copy to edit. `build.sh` copies it into the update skill.

## 0.5.2

- **Getting a new version:** go to Customize › Plugins, click the ⋮ next to The DREAM Engine and choose Check for updates. Then click The DREAM Engine and Update. Your guide tells you what's new the first time you use it after.

## 0.5.1

- **Selling on Teachers Pay Teachers?** Your guide now knows TPT's own tools and rules: the Keyword Finder, Search Analytics, bundles, sales, your weekly Note to Followers, and what you can and can't link to.
- **Pinterest timing:** your guide plans seasonal pins 4 to 6 weeks ahead, and shows you how to schedule pins up to 30 days in advance.

## 0.5.0

- **Market is here.** Your guide now helps you get your product in front of the right people, without social media: your marketing map, where to market (Etsy search, Pinterest, an email list, people you know, communities and partners, plus more marketplaces like Amazon KDP, Printify, Teachers Pay Teachers and Creative Market), what to say, the setup built in your Canva, and your first 30 days.
- **Then weekly check-ins:** see what's working, and change one thing at a time.
- **Page checks and sales calls:** your guide checks your listing or page like a buyer would and hands you the 3 fixes that help most. Selling on a call or a live? It plans it with you, with a practice call first.
- **A clearer first step:** Define now shows where you are (like "Define · 2 of 7"), always puts its top pick first so you can just say yes, and gives you easy choices whenever you're stuck.
- **Honest feedback:** your guide now tells you kindly when something won't work, and why, based on Kristin's method or real research. It won't just agree with you, and it shows you the better way.
- **Stronger first lines:** your guide now writes hooks for your pins, posts and launch messages with Kristin's hooks, in your buyer's own words.

## 0.4.3

- **Building on Skool?** Your guide now follows Kristin's Skool method: your name and look, a classroom that's easy to find your way around, welcome messages people answer, an About page that sells, and live calls.

## 0.4.2

- **Making a course?** Your guide now follows Kristin's course-building method: right-sizing it, outlining it with you, action sheets, slides, a "Start here" lesson and simple recording tips. On Skool too.

## 0.4.1

- **A new name:** your guide is now called the DREAM Engine. Same steps, same notes, nothing for you to do.

## 0.4.0

- **Stan Store, Beacons, Payhip and Gumroad:** the words for your product page now come ready for each box, as plain text.
- **Only the pictures your shop can use:** 2 on Stan, 8 to 10 on Etsy, all made in your Canva.
- **The email buyers get after they pay:** written for you, with the clicks to set it up on your platform.
- **Updates:** after you update your guide, it tells you what's new the first time you use it.
