# Delivering the product, step by step

Read this in Apply Round 8 (choosing how buyers get it) and Round 9 (setting it up on their platform), and again for an add-on.
Walk them through their route **3 to 5 steps per message,** then wait for "done" or a screenshot, like every platform setup.
Facts were checked on each platform's own pages in October 2026. Screens change: if what they see doesn't match, ask what they see and help from there.

**The rule behind every route:** the buyer gets exactly the delivery option the student chose in Round 8 (a file, a Canva view link, a template link, a thank-you PDF, or Google Drive), and exactly what the listing promised. Upload a file when they chose a file. Paste a link when they chose a link.

---

## 1. Pick their route

| Where they sell | They chose a file (PDF, images) | They chose a link (Canva view link or template link) |
|---|---|---|
| **Etsy** | Route A: upload to the listing (up to 5 files, 20 MB each). Too big, or more files? Route C. | Route C: the link goes inside a thank-you PDF, and they upload that PDF. Etsy listings deliver files, not links. |
| **Stan Store** | Route A: upload to the product. | Route E: paste it as the product's link. |
| **Beacons** | Route A: upload to the product (up to 6 files). | Route E: paste it as the redirect link. |
| **Payhip** | Route A: upload to the product (up to 5 GB). | Route C: inside a thank-you PDF. |
| **Gumroad** | Route A: the product's Content tab. | Route C: inside a thank-you PDF. |
| **Skool** | Route A: a resource file in a classroom lesson (`skool.md`, Step 4). | Route E: the link inside the lesson. |
| **Systeme.io (sold in a funnel)** | Route B (a Google Drive link) or Route D (a Systeme.io course). | Route E: the link on the thank-you page and in the delivery email. |
| **A bundle or several items, anywhere** | Route C: a thank-you PDF. | Route C: a thank-you PDF. |

## 2. Get it ready

**When they chose a file:**

1. **Export it for them** with the Canva connector, in the format from Execute's save table (printables: PDF Print; ebooks and digital planners: PDF Standard).
2. **Name it with their main keyword,** with hyphens: `Budget-Planner-Printable.pdf`. Buyers see the file name.
3. **Check it:**
   - the page count matches the plan
   - the text is real text (not a picture of the page)
   - the size fits their platform's limit
4. **Hand it over:**
   - **If you can save files on their computer:** put it in their folder and tell them where.
   - **Otherwise:** give them the export's download link and tell them to download it now, because the link expires after a few hours.
   - **If you can't export at all:** give them the Canva clicks from Execute's save table.

**When they chose a link:**
- **Canva public view link** (Canva's help):
  1. Open the design.
  2. **Share** → **Public view link** → **Create public view link**.
  3. Name it, then **Save and copy**.
- **Canva template link** (needs Canva Pro): **Share** → **See all** → **Template link** → **Create template link** → **Copy** (Execute's save table).
- **Then check the link before using it:**
  - It opens the right design. Expand Canva shortlinks with the Canva connector.
  - It works signed out.
  - A view link: what the buyer can do with it (view, print, download).
  - A template link: it makes a fresh copy.
- Make sure the listing says exactly what they'll get.

## Route A: upload the file to the platform

Hand them the file, then walk them through their platform:

**Stan Store** (Stan's help center, checked October 4, 2026; the full steps are in `storefronts.md`, Stan Store):
1. **My Store** → **Add Product** → **Digital Product**.
2. **Thumbnail tab:** the **Callout** style (Kristin's pick), the 400 x 400 image, title, subtitle and button text (Round 3's words).
3. **Checkout Page tab:** the 1920 x 1080 banner, the description, the price.
4. **At the bottom of the Checkout Page, upload the PDF.** That's the upload option, not "redirect to a URL", which is for links.
5. **Options** → **Confirmation Email:** paste the subject and message from `buyer-email.md`. Stan adds the product to the email itself.
6. **Publish**, preview it as a customer would, then connect payments (Stan only allows that once a product is published).

**What the buyer gets on Stan:** a thank-you page with direct access to the file, plus a confirmation email with the product included.

**Beacons** (Kristin's steps):
1. **Store** → **Add New Item**.
2. **Upload the PDF** (up to 6 files). Choose the upload, not the redirect link.
3. Name, price, short description, thumbnail, then extra images.
4. **Publish**, then add it to their page: **+ Add Products** → choose it.
5. Preview the product and checkout. If their plan has discount codes, run a test order with a 100%-off code to see exactly what the buyer receives.

**Etsy:** in the listing form's **Item details** → **Digital files**, upload the PDF (`storefronts.md`, Etsy).
- File names can't be changed after upload, so rename first.
- Use **Preview** to check, because sellers can't buy from their own shop.

**Payhip:** **Products** → **Add Digital Product** → **Upload product file**.

**Gumroad:** the product's **Content** tab → add the file. Check it with Gumroad's test purchase.

**Skool:** the course → the lesson → **Edit** → **ADD** → **Add resource file**.

## Route E: a link (Canva view link or template link)

**For:** students who chose to deliver with a link. Paste the link where the platform takes one. Where it doesn't, put it inside a thank-you PDF (Route C).
- **Stan Store:** in the product step, choose the URL option instead of uploading, and paste the link. The confirmation email includes it.
- **Beacons:** paste it as the product's redirect link, instead of uploading a file.
- **Systeme.io:** the thank-you page's button and the delivery email (build both for them, `storefronts.md`).
- **Skool:** add the link inside the lesson.
- **Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad:** inside a thank-you PDF (Route C), uploaded as the product file.

**Tell them, one line each:**
- **A view link:**
  - It always shows their latest version, so updates reach every buyer.
  - Anyone who has it can open it.
  - If they delete the design or the link, buyers lose access.
- **A template link:** each buyer gets their own copy, and the original stays safe. Never an "Anyone can edit" link (Kristin's warning).

## Route B: a Google Drive link

**For:** Systeme.io funnels (Systeme.io can't attach a file to a product sold in a funnel), files too big for the platform, and the files behind a thank-you PDF.

It's easiest on a computer. Their steps (Google's own help: https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494822):
1. Go to **drive.google.com** and sign in.
2. **New** → **File upload** → choose the PDF. Wait until the upload finishes.
3. Right-click the file → **Share** → **Share**.
4. Under **General access**, change **Restricted** to **Anyone with the link**.
5. Keep the role as **Viewer**.
6. Leave downloading on: in the share window's settings (the gear), keep the option that lets viewers download, print and copy. Buyers need the download button.
7. **Copy link** → **Done**, and paste the link to you.

**Then you check it before using it:**
- The link opens **the right file** (check the title on page 1). Students mix up links easily, and in Kristin's own test the first link pasted was the wrong product.
- It opens **while signed out**, with a download button. If you can't open it yourself, ask them to paste it into a private or incognito browser window and tell you what they see.

**Where the link goes:**
- **Systeme.io:** the thank-you page's download button and the delivery email. Build both for them (`storefronts.md`, Systeme.io).
- **A thank-you PDF:** one button per file (Route C).

**Things to tell them, one line each:**
- **Anyone who has the link can open the file.** That's normal for a low-price printable. For pricier products, use Route D.
- **Don't move, rename into another folder, or delete the file,** or every buyer's link breaks.
- **To update the file later without breaking the link,** upload the new version over the old one: in the file's menu, look for **Manage versions** → **Upload new version**.

## Route C: a thank-you (access) PDF

**For:** bundles, several files, template packs, or files over the platform's limit.
1. Build the one-page thank-you PDF in Canva, in their product's look (Round 8 says what goes on it).
2. Each button opens one item:
   - a Google Drive link to a PDF (Route B)
   - a Canva template link
   - a "make a copy" link to a Google Sheet
3. Export it as **PDF Standard,** so the buttons stay clickable.
4. Upload that PDF itself to their platform (Route A).
5. Check every button in a signed-out or private window.

## Route D: a Systeme.io course (more protected)

The buyer has to log in to download, so a forwarded link doesn't give the file away. It takes more setup. **Not tested in Kristin's account yet:** say so if they choose it, and check every step with a test purchase.
1. **You build it with the connector:**
   - a course, with one module and one lesson called "Download your {product name}"
   - the product sold in the funnel gives access to that course
2. **They add the PDF:**
   - Open the lesson in Systeme.io's editor.
   - Add a file or download element and upload the PDF.
   - Save.

   The connector can't attach files, so this click is theirs. Ask what they see.
3. **You write the thank-you page and the delivery email** to say exactly where to go: "Log in to your course to download your planner." Use the course link.
4. **Run a test purchase** with a 100%-off coupon. Check the buyer can log in, open the lesson and download the PDF.

---

## The final check, every route (Round 11)

Look at it the way a buyer will, on a phone:
- **The right product:** the product name is on page 1, and every page is there.
- **The delivery they chose, matching the listing:**
  - A PDF downloads and prints.
  - A view link opens the design while signed out.
  - A template link makes the buyer their own copy.
- **The add-on, if they have one,** arrives only for buyers who chose it, in the delivery option chosen for it.
