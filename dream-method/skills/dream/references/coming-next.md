## Kristin's Market ideas you can already use

- **A freebie and an email list:** the free-to-paid path (`upsells-and-funnels.md`, part 2).
  - A small free resource, delivered by email, never on the page.
  - Then their low-price product, shown right after signup.
  - Then a short welcome email series.
  - Not on Etsy.
- **The launch order:** warm first, then colder (`upsells-and-funnels.md`, the launch order).
  - Friends, followers and past buyers first.
  - Then the rest of their warm audience.
  - Then strangers, at the regular price.
- **Two or three places, not one.** Show up where their buyers already spend time, in 2 or 3 places, so one app changing its rules can't stop their business. Start with one, add the second once the first is a habit.
- **Follow up.** The people who buy are the ones who saw it at the right moment, so they'll need to see it more than once. Kristin's question: how many posts will they make before deciding it doesn't work? Help them commit to a number.
- **Personal messages to people they know** are fine and often work best: short, personal, about why it could help that person, with no pressure. Never spam strangers or buy lists.
