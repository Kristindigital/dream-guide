# Execute blueprints: how to build each product

One blueprint for each of Kristin's 8 product types.
Read the one that matches their product before you plan the pages. Use it as your plan; the student never has to read it.

Every blueprint follows the same three ideas from `why-people-buy.md`:
- **Start fast:** a "How to use this" page at the front, with a filled-in example wherever one helps.
- **One promise:** every page helps the promise in their notes come true. If a page doesn't, cut it.
- **A first version, not a forever version:** build the size given below. More pages can be version 2 or a bundle.

## Things every product needs

- **Cover:** product name, who it's for, and the promise in a few words.
- **How to use this:** 3 to 5 short steps, written for a tired beginner.
- **A consistent look:** one palette, two fonts, the same header style on every page.
- **About me:** a page just before "What's next". It has a photo spot (their photo or logo, optional) and 3 or 4 warm sentences:
  - who they are
  - why they made this, from their own experience in their notes
  - where to find more from them
  This is often the reason a buyer trusts a brand new seller.
  **Never invent credentials, titles or experience** ("certified", "expert", "10 years"). Use only what's in their notes, and leave a marked spot for anything missing.
  That includes their story. Feelings, struggles and results ("the money was always gone by the 20th", "we finally paid every bill on time") must come from their own words. If a line would help but isn't in their notes, ask first: "Is it true that...? Want me to add it?"
  In a very short product (a 1 to 3 page checklist), fold it into 2 lines on the closing page instead. Mini-courses keep it as slide 3 (Kristin's order).
- **A closing page:** "What's next" (one simple next step), their shop or social name, and a thank you.
- **Their name** or shop name on the cover.

## Licensing (check before you build)

- **Canva elements are fine inside a design** (a planner, an ebook, a template), following Canva's Content License.
- **Never sell Canva's own icons, illustrations or photos as separate files.** That rules out graphics kits made from Canva's library. Kit art has to be theirs (see blueprint 4).
- **Canva Pro elements in a template pack** show a watermark to buyers on a free Canva account. Use free elements, or the listing must say "Canva Pro needed".
- **Large Pro photos in ebooks:** Canva limits unedited Pro photos in e-books. Keep photos small, use free photos, or use their own.
- **Fonts:** buyers can't be given Canva's font files. A kit names its fonts; a template uses fonts that are in Canva.
- **AI images they make** (for example with Canva's AI image tools) are theirs to use, as long as they didn't start from a Canva library image. Never describe AI art as hand-drawn.

## 1. Printables and planners

Habit trackers, journals, budget sheets, checklists, worksheets, meal planners.

- **Build in:** Canva.
- **First version:** 8 to 15 pages. A checklist can be 1 to 3 pages.
- **Order:** Cover · How to use this (with a filled-in example) · the core pages · a short review or reflection page if it fits · About me · What's next.
- **Each core page:** one job per page. Say whether it's daily, weekly or monthly.
- **Shapes that work:**
  - Habit tracker: a 31-day grid, 5 to 8 habits, a monthly review box.
  - Budget sheet: income, bills, spending and savings tables, with a total line on each.
  - Meal planner: a week across, a matching grocery list, a "foods we already like" list.
  - Journal: one prompt per page, with real writing space.
  - Worksheet: a question, an example answer in grey, then space to write.
  - Checklist: grouped items with tick boxes, short enough to fit on one page.
- **Design:**
  - Writing lines at least 1 cm (about 3/8 inch) apart. Boxes big enough for real handwriting.
  - No tiny text. Labels, table headers, day names and helper lines are at least 10 pt, so they're easy to read once printed.
  - Everything at least 1.5 cm (about half an inch) from the edge, so home printers don't cut it off.
  - Light backgrounds and no full-page dark colors, so it's kind to their buyer's ink.
  - It should still look good printed in black and white.
- **Canva format:** US Letter or A4, portrait.
- **Save as:** PDF Print.
- **Grow it later:** Kristin's tip: bundle 3 to 5 related printables into one bigger product.

## 2. Ebooks and guides

How-to PDFs, step-by-step guides, mini workbooks.

- **Selling on Etsy?** Etsy doesn't allow instructions written by AI, so the steps must be in their own words (`step-execute.md`, Etsy check).

- **Build in:** Canva up to about 20 pages. Longer, text-heavy guides: your own PDF.
- **First version:** 15 to 30 pages, with 3 to 7 short chapters.
- **Order (Kristin's mini-book):** Cover · Welcome · How to use this mini-book · Contents (if it's over 12 pages) · the chapters · a printable list or checklist · About me · What's next.
- **Welcome page:** who it's for, the one thing they'll be able to do by the end, and a warm line from the student.
- **Each chapter:** a short opening, the steps in easy sections, one practical tip or real example, an "Action step" box, and a one-line takeaway.
- **Mini workbook:** after each chapter, add a fill-in page with prompts and writing space.
- **Design:**
  - Body text big enough to read on a phone screen.
  - The product name and chapter title in a small header on every page. Page numbers.
  - Tips and key points in a highlighted box. One pull quote per chapter, at most.
  - Short paragraphs, lots of white space. No wall of text.
- **Canva format:** US Letter or A4, portrait.
- **Save as:** PDF Standard. If it's a workbook made for printing, PDF Print.
- **Kristin's tip:** the "What's next" page points to their next product, or their free download.

## 3. Canva template packs

Social media, business or content templates that buyers edit in Canva.

- **Build in:** Canva, always.
- **First version:** 20 to 30 templates is Kristin's sweet spot. 10 to 15 is fine for a first version.
- **Needs Canva Pro** to create the template link buyers receive.
- **The pack:**
  - A "Start here" design: how to open the templates, change text, swap photos and recolor everything in a few clicks.
  - Templates grouped by what they're for (for example posts, stories, carousels, highlight covers). Each one named for its job ("Client win post", "Quote post", "Launch story").
  - A short About me at the end of the "Start here" design.
  - A one-page **access PDF** with a big "Open your templates" link and 3 quick steps. Shops like Etsy deliver a file, so this file is how buyers get the link.
- **Design:**
  - Every text box clearly says what goes there ("Your headline here").
  - Photo spots are obvious frames.
  - One color system across the whole pack, so a buyer can rebrand it fast.
  - Free Canva elements wherever possible (see Licensing).
- **Canva format:** the size each platform needs (for example Instagram post, Instagram story, Pinterest pin). Each template as its own page in one design, or its own design.
- **Save as:** a template link (Share → See all → Template link). The access PDF is saved as PDF Standard.

## 4. Graphics and brand kits

Icon sets, backgrounds, color palettes, design elements, clip art, SVGs.

- **Build in:** the showcase in Canva (or your own PDF). The elements are their own art files.
- **First rule:** every element must be theirs: drawn by them, or made by them with an AI image tool whose terms let them sell it. Never Canva's library elements (see Licensing). If they can't make original art, steer them gently to a template pack or printable instead.
- **First version:** 20 to 50 elements, 1 to 5 color palettes with hex codes, one font pairing, and a usage guide. One clear style (boho, minimal, bold, playful).
- **Order (the showcase PDF: Kristin's six pages, plus About me):** Kit cover · Color palettes with hex codes · Font pairing in use · All the elements in a neat grid · Backgrounds · How to use this kit · About me.
- **The files:** elements as PNG with a transparent background, backgrounds as JPG, and SVG versions for Cricut and other cutting machines (needs Canva Pro).
- **Fonts:** name them (with where to find them) rather than including font files.
- **Your part:** plan the kit, write the AI prompts if they're using one, name every file clearly, and build the showcase PDF.
- **Save as:** the showcase as PDF Standard, plus the element files.

## 5. AI prompt packs

Ready-to-use prompts for one specific kind of person.

- **Not on Etsy.** Etsy bans AI prompt packs (`storefronts.md`, Etsy). Sell them on Stan, Payhip, Gumroad, Beacons or Systeme.io.

- **Build in:** your own PDF (keeps 30 to 50 prompts neat and consistent). Canva if they want to edit it there.
- **First version:** 30 to 50 prompts in 4 to 6 categories, about 10 to 15 pages.
- **Test every prompt yourself before it goes in.** Kristin's rule: only include ones that work. Cut or fix anything that gives a weak answer.
- **Order:** Cover · How to use this pack (copy it, fill in the [brackets], paste it into an AI chat) · one worked example with a filled-in prompt and a short sample answer · one page per category · About me · What's next.
- **Each prompt:** a number, a one-line "what it does", then the prompt itself in its own box.
- **Design:**
  - [Brackets] in a highlight color.
  - Clean, very readable font. Buyers will come back to this again and again.
  - Category headers that are easy to spot when scrolling.
- **Canva format:** US Letter or A4, portrait.
- **Save as:** PDF Standard.

## 6. Swipe files and copy packs

Done-for-you captions, email sequences, bio templates, scripts.

- **Build in:** your own PDF, or Canva if they want to edit it there.
- **First version:** 20 to 50 pieces in 3 to 5 sections. Or a full sequence (for example a 5-email welcome series).
- **Order:** Cover · How to use (plus 3 tips for making it sound like them) · the sections · About me · What's next.
- **Each piece:** a number, what it's for, then the text with [brackets] to personalize.
- **Honesty inside the copy:** no fake results, no made-up testimonials, no income claims (`rules.md`). The copy should be something any buyer can use truthfully.
- **Design:** each piece in its own bordered box, [brackets] highlighted, a simple divider between pieces.
- **Canva format:** US Letter or A4, portrait.
- **Save as:** PDF Standard. Kristin's option: also share it as a Google Doc so buyers can copy it easily.

## 7. Digital planners

Interactive planners used on a tablet or phone, in apps like GoodNotes or Notability.

- **Build in:** your own PDF when there are lots of linked tabs. Canva for a small, simple one.
- **First version:** a cover, a contents page and 4 to 8 sections (for example: year at a glance, monthly, weekly, daily, notes, trackers). Undated is easiest for a first version, because it never goes out of date.
- **Order:** Cover with a "Start here" button · How to use (which apps it works in, how to import it, how the tabs work) · Contents · the sections · notes pages · About me.
- **Design:**
  - Tablet size, landscape is the usual choice.
  - Tabs down the side on every page, each linked to its section.
  - Big tap areas, large clear text, fill-in areas with a soft background.
- **Before saving:** click through every tab to check the links work.
- **Notion:** a Notion planner is built in Notion, not Canva. Offer the Canva version as their first product.
- **Save as:** PDF Standard (keeps the links working).

## 8. Mini-courses and workshops

Short lessons on one skill, as slides, a PDF, or video.

**Read `course-building.md` first:** right-size it, outline it with them in rounds, keep their words verbatim (they only teach what they've done), add action sheets only where there's a deliverable, and follow its slide rules.

- **Selling on Etsy?** The lessons must be in their own words, never written by AI (Etsy's AI rules, `storefronts.md`). Keeping their words verbatim already does this.

- **Build in:** Canva (a presentation).
- **First version:** 3 to 5 lessons, each teaching one thing, with an action step after each.
- **Order (Kristin's slides):** Welcome (title, their name, a one-line promise) · What you'll learn (3 to 5 outcomes) · About me · the lessons, one idea per slide · Your action step · Congratulations and next steps.
- **A bonus that fits:** a short printable workbook with the action steps (use blueprint 2).
- **Video:** if they'll record it, write their talking points in each slide's speaker notes. They talk over the slides in Canva or any screen recorder.
- **Design:** one idea per slide, key words highlighted, the same banner or header on every slide.
- **Canva format:** Presentation (16:9).
- **Save as:** PDF Standard for buyers, PowerPoint if they'll present it live, MP4 if they record it.
