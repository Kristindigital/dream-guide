# The email buyers get after they pay

Read this in Apply **Round 9, before the product is published,** so the very first buyer gets it. Read it again for an add-on, and in Round 11 to test it.
Facts were checked on each platform's own help pages on October 5, 2026. Screens change: if what they see doesn't match, ask what they see and help from there.

Tell them why in one line:
> This is the first message a buyer gets from you after paying. A warm, clear one makes them feel looked after, and it's where they'll find their {product} again later.

---

## 1. Who does what

- **The platform adds the download itself** on Stan, Beacons, Payhip, Gumroad and Etsy. Their words go around it. Never add a second download link there.
- **On Systeme.io the email is the delivery.** You build it with the download button or link from Round 8 (`storefronts.md`, Systeme.io).
- **Some platforms have one email for each product, and some have one for the whole shop:**

| Platform | One email per… | So write… |
|---|---|---|
| Stan Store, Beacons, Gumroad, Systeme.io | product | the product version |
| Etsy, Payhip | whole shop | the shop version: shop words only, no single product name (`shop-look.md`, "Shop words vs product words") |

## 2. Write it for them

Fill it in from their notes, in their voice (`voice.md`), at a 4th or 5th grade reading level, short enough to read on a phone.
- **Their story line** comes only from their own words in their notes. Never invent one.
- **Their email address:** if they'd rather not type it in the chat, leave `{your email}` for them to fill in on the platform.
- **Show it, get their OK, then hand it over box by box,** labeled with their platform's box names (section 3), each in its own copy box.

### The product version (Stan, Beacons, Gumroad, Systeme.io)

> **Subject:** Your {product name} is here
>
> Hi there,
>
> Thank you for buying the {product name}! {One warm line from their notes: why they made it, in their words.}
>
> **Your download:** {where it is on their platform, section 3}. Save it to your computer or phone now, so it's there whenever you need it.{Beacons only: " The link works for 7 days."}
>
> **For the best start:** {one tip from their product's How to Use page}.
>
> **Need help?** If anything won't open, email me at {their email} and I'll make it right.
>
> {The review line, see below.}
>
> {Optional, only if true: "I'm making more {niche} printables. You can find them at {their shop link}."}
>
> {Their sign-off},
> {Their name}, {shop name}

**The review line:**
- **Where buyers can leave a review on the product** (Etsy and Gumroad, for example): "If it makes {the result, in the buyer's words} a little easier, I'd love an honest review."
- **Where they can't, or you're not sure** (on Stan, the reviews in the Options tab may be ones the seller adds): "I'd love to hear how it goes. Just email me." Their reply can become a real testimonial later, only with their permission (`rules.md`, rule 3).

### The shop version (Etsy, Payhip)

The same, with three changes:
- **Subject (Payhip):** "Your {shop name} order is here".
- **First line:** "Thank you for your order from {shop name}!", plus one warm line about their whole niche.
- **The tip** must fit everything they sell, now and later. For printables: "Print at 100% (Actual size) on regular letter paper." For templates: "Make your own copy before you edit."

### Sam's, filled in (an example of the product version on Stan)

> **Subject:** Your Picky Eater Meal Planner is here
>
> Hi there,
>
> Thank you for buying the Picky Eater Meal Planner! I made it because I was so tired of cooking two dinners every night, and I wanted one simple plan the whole family could eat.
>
> **Your download:** your planner is in this email. Save the PDF to your computer now, so you can print a fresh week any time.
>
> **For the best start:** fill in "Foods My Kids Will Eat" first, then plan your week from that list.
>
> **Need help?** If anything won't open, email me at {your email} and I'll make it right.
>
> I'd love to hear how your first week goes. Just email me.
>
> Happy dinners,
> Sam, OneDinnerMama

### Keep it honest

- **No promised results** ("your kids will eat everything"). Say what the product does, not what it guarantees.
- **Reviews: ask for an honest one, never offer anything in return,** and never write one for them (`rules.md`, rule 3). On Etsy, this is also Etsy's rule.
- **No fake urgency,** and no "what's next" hint unless it's real.
- **Every link in it opens the right page.** Check each one before they save it.

## 3. Set it up, platform by platform

Walk them through 3 to 5 steps per message, as always.

### Stan Store (Stan's help, article 191)
**Do it in Round 9, step 5, before they publish the product.**
1. **My Store** → open the product.
2. **Options** tab → scroll down to **Confirmation Email**.
3. Paste the subject, then the message.
4. **Publish** (or **Save As Draft**).

Good to know:
- It's per product.
- **Stan adds the product to the email itself:** the file, or the link if they chose "redirect to a URL". In the template, "your planner is in this email" is right.
- The Stan logo stays on the email; it can't be removed.
- PDFs can't be attached. An extra document (not the product) needs a shareable link.
- The email comes from **orders@stan.store**. Buyers also get a separate payment receipt from Stripe.

Source: https://help.stan.store/article/191-how-can-i-customize-the-email-my-customer-receives-after-opting-in-to-a-product

### Beacons (Beacons' help, "How to Set up Your Beacons Store in Minutes")
1. In the product's settings, find **Personalize your confirmation email.** Beacons' help lists it with the product's settings but doesn't give the clicks, so ask what they see.
2. Paste the message (and the subject, if there's a box for it).
3. Save, then publish.

Good to know:
- It's per product, and Beacons adds the download link.
- **Download links only work for 7 days after purchase** (Beacons' help, "How will customers access my digital products?"). That's why the Beacons email says "Save it now" and "The link works for 7 days."
- Follow-up emails after a purchase (Email Marketing app → **Automations** → "After someone purchases a product") come later, in Market.

Sources: https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4699393 and https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4699713

### Payhip (Payhip's help, "Email Receipts")
1. **Account** → **Settings** → **Advanced Settings** → scroll down to **Email Receipt**.
2. Fill in **Subject** and **Message**.
3. Click **Save Settings** at the bottom.

Good to know:
- **One email for every product in their store,** so use the shop version.
- Payhip adds the download link.
- Payhip's article is from 2021. If the menu looks different, ask what they see.

Source: https://help.payhip.com/article/100-email-receipts

### Gumroad (Gumroad's blog, "Customizable receipts", January 12, 2026)
1. Open the product → the **Receipt** tab.
2. Add the custom message.
3. Change the button text to the benefit words ("Download my planner").
4. Save.

It's per product, and Gumroad adds the download button.
Source: https://gumroad.com/blog/p/customizable-receipts

### Etsy (Etsy's help, "How to Add or Edit Your Post-Purchase Message to Buyers")
Etsy has **two** messages, and both apply to the whole shop:
1. **Shop Manager** → **Settings** → **Info & Appearance**.
2. **Message to Buyers:** shown on the receipt page and in the order confirmation email. Keep it short: the thank-you, and the help line.
3. **Message to Buyers for Digital Items:** shown on the Downloads page of digital orders. Put the tip, "save your files now", the help line and the review line here.
4. **Save Changes.**

Good to know:
- Use the shop version, because both messages apply to every listing.
- **No links to other shops or websites.** Etsy doesn't allow sending buyers elsewhere to buy. Their Etsy shop link is fine.
- Do it once, in Round 9, before their first listing goes live.

Source: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000337107

### Systeme.io (you build it)
- **The email is the delivery:** the subject and message from the template, plus the download button or link from Round 8 (`delivery.md`, Route B or E).
- Build it with the connector as an automation email, plus a rule: new sale on the order form → send that email (`storefronts.md`, Systeme.io, step 5). Show it before saving.
- It goes out under their default sender name, so sign it to match.
- An add-on gets its own email, sent by its tag rule (`upsells-and-funnels.md`).

### Skool
No purchase email to write. Skool sends its own notifications, and the welcome happens inside the community: AutoDM or a personal message (`skool.md`).

### Any other platform (ThriveCart, Kajabi, Podia and others)
Look for the email buyers get after paying. It may be called a confirmation, receipt or fulfillment email. Look up the platform's current help, or ask what they see. Never guess menu names.

## 4. Check it (Round 11)

Open the email the way a buyer will, on a phone:
- The download works, and it's the right product.
- Every link opens the right page.
- Their name, shop name and email are right.

**How to see it:**
- **Stan Store** (Stan's help, article 233), before they share their store:
  1. Set the product's price to **$0** (**Checkout Page** → **Price**) and **Publish**.
  2. Open their store link, and "buy" it with their own email.
  3. Check the thank-you page and the email from orders@stan.store (look in spam or promotions too).
  4. **Set the price back, and Publish again.**

  Never test by buying at full price and refunding: Stripe keeps its fees. Source: https://help.stan.store/article/233-testing-products-in-your-store
- **Gumroad:** its test purchase.
- **Etsy:** sellers can't buy from their own shop. Read both messages back in **Info & Appearance**, and use **Preview** for the listing.
- **Beacons, Payhip:** a 100%-off code, if their plan has discount codes. Otherwise, read the saved message back.
- **Systeme.io:** the 100%-off test coupon.
