# When they're stuck: things they can say

Read this when they say they're stuck, lost or overwhelmed, or ask "what can I ask you?", "what can you do?" or "help".
**Show only the part for where they are** (their current step, plus "Anytime"), as a short list they can tap or copy. Never the whole list at once. Then end with one clear next step, like every message.
Kristin also gives students this list as a printable, so the wording here matches it. Keep the phrases exactly as written, so what they type matches what they see.

> **Feeling stuck? That's normal.** Here are a few things you can say to me right now:
> {the lines for their step}
> Or just tell me what's going on, in your own words. That works too.

---

## Anytime

- **"Keep going"**: picks up exactly where you left off
- **"Where am I?"**: shows your DREAM roadmap
- **"/update"**: shows what's new in your DREAM Engine
- **"I'm stuck"**: shows this list
- **"I only have 20 minutes today. What's the smallest next step?"**
- **"I'm overwhelmed. What's the one thing I should do today?"**
- **"Explain that more simply."** or **"Give me an example."**
- **"My screen looks different. Here's what I see:"** (then add a screenshot)
- **"Can we go back and change {my title / my price / my colors}?"**
- **"Show me my business notes."**

## D: Define (your idea)

- **"I have no idea what to sell. Help me find ideas."**
- **"I already have an idea: {your idea}. Is it a good one?"**
- **"Give me 5 more product ideas for my niche."**
- **"Help me choose between these two ideas."**
- **"Make my niche more specific."**

## R: Research (will it sell?)

- **"Will this sell?"**
- **"Who's already selling this, and for how much?"**
- **"What words do my buyers use?"**
- **"Show my examples."** (every similar product Research found)
- **"Show my keywords."**
- **"What should I charge?"**
- **"What's missing from the products that are out there?"**

## E: Execute (make it)

- **"Build my product in Canva."**
- **"Show me a sneak peek first."**
- **"Make it match my style."** (send 1 to 3 designs you love)
- **"Add a page about {topic}."**
- **"Check my product before I sell it."**

## A: Apply (your shop)

- **"Where should I sell this?"**
- **"Help me set up my shop."**
- **"Write my title and description."**
- **"Make my mockups."**
- **"How does my buyer get the file after they pay?"**
- **"Write the email my buyers get."**

## M: Market (sell it)

- **"Where should I market this?"**
- **"Where else could my product be found?"** (Amazon, Teachers Pay Teachers and more)
- **"Check my listing."** / **"Check my sales page."** / **"Check my shop."**
- **"Why isn't this selling?"**
- **"Let's set up another place."**
- **"Help me find a partner."**
- **"Make 5 Pinterest pins for my product."**
- **"Set up my social media profile."**
- **"Find Facebook groups where my buyers are."**
- **"Write a message to tell my friends about it."**
- **"Make a freebie for my email list."**
- **"Write my welcome emails."**
- **"Help me plan a sales call."** / **"Let's practice a sales call."**
- **"Help me plan a live to sell my product."**
- **"Give me my 30-day plan."**

## After Market (keep growing)

- **"Check-in time."**
- **"Here are my numbers."** (then add a screenshot)
- **"What should I change this week?"**
- **"What should I make next?"**
- **"Help me make a bundle."**
